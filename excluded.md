# Excluded — dropped by the H-1B filter

These roles looked like a fit on title alone, and were **dropped on purpose**.
Keep this list so the filter stays honest.

## 1. CrowdStrike — Associate Security Engineer (Remote)
- Source: https://builtin.com/job/associate-security-engineer-remote/10779705
- Posted: 2026-08-24 · $70,000–$95,000
- Why dropped: "Due to federal government compliance and GovCloud infrastructure requirements, candidates must be a U.S. Citizen or U.S. Permanent Resident"
- Lesson: CrowdStrike *does* sponsor some roles, but GovCloud roles do not. Check the citizenship line on every CrowdStrike posting.
