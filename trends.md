# H-1B + PERM filing trends — Nov 13, 2026

Sources are USCIS, DOL OFLC, and DOL LCA disclosure data as reported
Nov 2026. Numbers are FY (Oct–Sep) unless stated.

## H-1B

- **FY2026 registrations:** 343,981 eligible, down 55% from 758,994 in FY2024, down 26.9% from FY2025. Beneficiary-centric selection (one entry per person) cut duplicate filings — registrations per beneficiary fell to 1.01.
- **FY2026 selection:** 120,141 selected, 34.9–35.3% selection rate, up from 24.8% in FY2024. 118,660 unique beneficiaries selected.
- **FY2025 approvals:** 406,347 H-1B petitions approved, 2.1% denied. Denial rates are near historic lows (2–3%, vs 15% in FY2018).
- **Who gets them:** Computer occupations = 63.9–64% of approvals. India = 71% of beneficiaries. California leads on approvals.
- **Top employers FY2025:** Amazon — 19,181 approvals (4,637 initial + 14,000+ continuing). 14 large IT services firms = 8.0% of approvals, down from 25.8% in FY2017.
- **Cap:** 85,000/year (65,000 regular + 20,000 US master's), unchanged since 2005.

### FY2027 — the big change

- **Wage-weighted lottery:** USCIS replaced the random lottery. Higher-paid registrations get more entries — Level IV (highest wage) gets 4× the weight of Level I.
- **$100,000 fee:** Proclamation 11069, Sep 18, 2026 — $100,000 fee for certain new H-1B petitions. 700+ petitions paid it in year one (per early reporting).
- **Outsourcer collapse:** Registrations from the largest IT staffing / outsourcing firms fell 92%, from 24,946 to 2,055. FY2027 total registrations fell ~38.5% year-on-year to ~211,600 (per Mordor / proclamation reporting).
- **What this means for KD:** good news, relatively. A mid/senior Security Engineer at $140k–$180k+ is weighted better than a Level I / low-wage filing. Target higher-wage, specialty roles (Detection, EDR, AI Security, Cloud Security) at product companies and banks, not low-wage staffing firms.

## PERM (green-card labor certification)

PERM is the DOL labor-market test an employer must clear before sponsoring
EB-2 / EB-3. It is *not* the H-1B. An employer can file H-1B and be blocked
on PERM, or vice versa.

- **FY2025 received:** 117,849 PERM applications, down ~22% from 151,773 in FY2024.
- **FY2025 decided:** 138,093 certified, 3,404 denied, 6,730 withdrawn (148,227 processed).
- **Pending:** 157,669 awaiting processing Sep 30, 2025 (down from 183,776 a year earlier). 84,672 still undecided as of Nov 13, 2026 per PERM Tracker.
- **Processing time:** Analyst review averaged ~300 days in Oct 2026 DOL data; ~336 days for determinations completed in Aug 2026. DOL was working February 2026 filings as of Nov 13, 2026.
- **FY2026 Q1–Q2:** 55,361 PERM applications submitted, almost flat vs FY2025 (+7 cases). 55,218 certified in H1 FY2026.
- **Who dominates:** Software Developers = 22.7–23.1% of certifications. Professional / Scientific / Technical Services = 27.8% of FY2025 certifications.
- **India EB-2 wait:** Final action date retrogressed to Sep 1, 2013 in the June 2026 visa bulletin. India per-country EB-2 limit reached May 22, 2026.

### PERM suspension — Oct 8, 2026

Eight employers suspended from PERM, no announced end date:
**Microsoft, Adobe, Cognizant Technology Solutions, Infosys, Tata, Wipro, HCL Technologies, Capgemini.**

DOL said the eight had received 230,000+ H-1B approvals and 100,000+ labor certifications since 2009. The IT services firms had largely stopped filing PERM before the freeze (TCS / Cognizant / Wipro / Capgemini combined: ~3,600 in FY2024 → ~100 in FY2026 through June). Microsoft + Adobe filed ~1,900 PERM applications Oct 2025–Jun 2026, mostly Microsoft — so the freeze bites hardest there.

**Implication for KD:** do not treat Microsoft / Cognizant etc. as a green-card path right now, even though their H-1B history is strong. Prioritise employers with *active* PERM + H-1B history (Amazon, JPMorgan, Citibank, Meta, Visa, Vanguard, Deloitte, CrowdStrike, Palo Alto, Google, Cisco, GM).

## Security / cyber H-1B — the niche KD is in

DOL LCA disclosure, Jul–Sep 2025 (Q4 FY2025), certified H-1B filings only:

- **580 employers filed 1,093 certified H-1B applications for security / cyber titles, median offered wage $141,378.**
- Security Engineer: **444 filings, median $148,150**
- Security Analyst: **201 filings, median $120,300**
- Top security sponsors: Ernst & Young 83 (analyst), Amazon.com Services 59 (53 engineer), Microsoft 25, Accenture 14, Cognizant 13, Citibank 12, JPMorgan Chase 12, Meta 11, Visa 7, Vanguard 7, AWS 7.

A filing shows an employer sponsored that kind of role — not that it is hiring today. That is why this wrapper checks the *live posting* against the sponsor list, instead of trusting either one alone.
